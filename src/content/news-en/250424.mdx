On **March 28 and 31, 2025**, we held a **New Student Welcome Event** at the Taki Plaza B2F event space over two days.

This event was planned to help ease the anxiety of incoming students before enrollment and to provide a relaxed space where they could casually connect with one another.  
Participants were free to join or leave at any time, and many students stopped by on their way home from their health checkups.

At the venue, students enjoyed board games, snacks, and drinks, naturally striking up conversations with one another.  
A total of **approximately 330 new students** participated over the two days, and some even formed groups that went out to eat together afterward, making the event a lively place for building new communities.  
We were also pleased to welcome international students, offering a glimpse into cross-cultural exchange.

As the Community Team’s first spring event, these two days were filled with smiles, new connections, and memorable encounters.  
Thank you very much to everyone who joined us!
