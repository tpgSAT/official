At Koudaisai 2025, Taki Plaza Gardener (TPG) hosted a board game café called **“TPG Cafe”** on the B2 floor of Taki Plaza.

<ArticleImage src={poster} alt="Event poster" />

The highlight of this event was, without a doubt, the wide variety of **board games**!  
Using the many games owned by TPG, we created a space that could be enjoyed by everyone—whether visiting alone or with family.

<ArticleImage
  src={boardGame}
  alt="Board games"
  caption="Board games prepared for the event"
/>

As refreshments to enjoy alongside the board games, we offered **canned drinks** and **coffee jelly**.  
The coffee jelly was topped with vanilla ice cream, making it a delicious treat that was very well received.

<ArticleImage src={CafeMenu} alt="Menu" caption="Menu items offered" />

Although this year’s Koudaisai had a somewhat unusual format, we welcomed approximately **240 visitors** in total.  
On both days, the B2 floor of Taki Plaza was filled with excitement and lively activity.
