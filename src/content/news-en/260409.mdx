We held a welcome event at the Taki Plaza B2 event space on March 30 and 31, 2026.

This event is organized with the aim of helping incoming students interact and get to know each other before enrollment, so they can get their university life off to a great start.

On the day of the event, new students who were meeting for the first time interacted through board games and enjoyed lively conversations, making the event a great success.

Approximately 500 new students participated over the two days, and among them, some groups were seen going out to eat together after the event.

Also, thanks to the participation of international students, it became a great opportunity for international exchange.

We hope that this event served as a helpful space for new students to build community and ease any anxieties.

Thank you to all the new students who participated!
