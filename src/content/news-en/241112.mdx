---
title: "We Held a Drawing Shiritori Contest"
description: "On November 12, the Community Team hosted a Drawing Shiritori Contest."
date: 2024-11-12
tags: ["Event Report"]
---

During the Drawing Shiritori Contest, participants and members of the TPG Community Team were divided into three teams and competed under three different rules:
“Normal Drawing Shiritori,” “Non-Dominant Hand Drawing Shiritori,” and “Drawing Shiritori Shuttle Run.”

International students also joined the event, allowing everyone to enjoy a fresh and unique drawing shiritori experience.
After the event, the large sheets of paper used during the contest were displayed next to the workshop room for about two weeks, so anyone could continue the drawing shiritori freely.

Thank you very much to everyone who participated in the event!
