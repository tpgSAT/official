On Tuesday, July 15, the Community Team hosted a ramen-making event called **“Taki Ramen.”**

At this event, participants worked together to prepare four different types of ramen: **niboshi (dried sardine), miso, salt, and mazemen**.  
Many ramen lovers joined the event, and the noodle-draining process in particular created a lot of excitement and energy.

Due to the positive response, there might even be a **second edition** of the event…?

Thank you very much to everyone who participated!
